The Spark

How the mission began

What began as a desire to inspire children through joy, play, and meaningful gift-giving grew into a movement dedicated to uplifting young lives in underserved communities.

Pink Women on Fire was established on April 14, 2013, carrying a foundation of faith, love, and service.

A New Chapter

From Pink Women on Fire to The P.I.N.K. Incorporated

As the mission expanded, the organization stepped into a renewed identity that reflects intention, compassion, and deeper purpose.

P.I.N.K. stands for Purpose-driven, Inspiring change, Nurturing growth, and Kindness in action, with Bernice Garcia joining as Co-Founder in this next stage of growth.

The journey has moved from "Reaching the Unreachable, One Child at a Time" toward "Bringing the Unreachable Closer Together."

Our Work In Action

The profile highlights recurring themes in the organization’s field work. This section gives those themes a dedicated place so the website does more than list events, it explains the meaning behind them.

Messenger of Blessing

Giving essentials with dignity

What started as small items grew into bags of school supplies and basic essentials, shared through the support of individuals and partner groups.

Voice of the Unheard

Listening to communities often unseen

The work includes cultural immersions and community exchanges in places many people never get to see, helping families feel heard, recognized, and valued.

Sharing of Dreams

Encouraging children to hope

A core part of each celebration is creating space for children to dream, speak from the heart, and believe that education and hope can shape their future.

Community Partnership
